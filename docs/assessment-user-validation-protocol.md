# Assessment and tool user-validation protocol

Status: protocol ready; field testing not yet conducted.  
Version: 1.0 · 11 October 2026

This protocol is an execution plan, not research evidence. No result, benchmark, usability claim or validation claim may be published until sessions are actually completed and analysed.

## Scope

Test one representative flow from each audience and product family:

1. Class 10 route finder.
2. Class 11–12 assessment and Class 12 route filter.
3. Graduate/early-professional assessment.
4. Working-professional assessment.
5. Skill self-test and portfolio tracker.
6. Course eligibility finder and education payback planner.
7. Parent/student agreement worksheet.

Use a small formative round first: at least three participants per relevant audience for discovery, followed by a separate confirmation round only after the first-round issues are fixed. Treat school-age participants as minors: obtain guardian permission, participant assent, and collect no unnecessary identity or assessment answers.

## Session tasks

Ask the participant to think aloud while they:

- state the decision they are trying to make;
- complete the assigned assessment or tool without coaching;
- explain the result in their own words;
- identify one claim they would verify on an official source;
- choose one experiment or next action;
- download or review the report where applicable;
- say what they expected, what confused them, and what they would change.

Do not lead the participant toward a preferred route, answer, score or purchase. Stop if the participant becomes distressed or discloses sensitive information that is not needed for the task.

## Record only these measures

- task completed: yes/no;
- time to first meaningful action and total task time;
- wrong turns, dead ends and requests for clarification;
- whether the participant can accurately explain the result’s limits;
- whether the participant can identify the next action without prompting;
- one short participant quote, stripped of identifying details;
- facilitator severity: blocking, confusing, minor or none.

Keep notes in an access-controlled local file using participant IDs only. Do not upload raw notes, recordings, names, contact details, answers or minors’ data to the public site. Delete raw session material according to the approved retention period. Publish only aggregated findings after consent, review and an agreed minimum sample.

## Decision gates

Do not describe a feature as validated from a formative round. A change may be marked “ready for another round” only when blocking issues are fixed and the facilitator can reproduce the result. A research report or benchmark requires a defined population, sampling method, consent basis, analysis method, version, limitations and an owner for updates.

## Ready-to-run session sheet

Use one local sheet per session. The facilitator may copy this structure into an access-controlled file, but must not place completed sheets in the repository or public dataset.

```text

Audience/product family: ____________________   Round: formative / confirmation
Guardian permission required: yes / no   Assent recorded: yes / no / not applicable

Decision the participant is trying to make: __________________________________________
Assigned task: ______________________________________________________________________
Task completed: yes / no   Time to first meaningful action: ______   Total time: ______
Wrong turns or dead ends: ____________________________________________________________
Clarification requests: ______________________________________________________________
Could the participant explain the result limits without prompting? yes / partly / no
Could the participant name the next action without prompting? yes / partly / no
Official source or claim they chose to verify: ________________________________________
Participant quote with identifying details removed: ____________________________________
Severity: blocking / confusing / minor / none
Immediate safety or distress stop used: yes / no
Facilitator follow-up issue ID: ______________________________________________________
```

Before each session, the facilitator records only the session ID and assigned flow. Afterward, remove free-text details that could identify a person, store the sheet under the approved retention rule, and copy only issue IDs and aggregate counts into the analysis summary. The minimum analysis summary is: participants completing the task, median total time, count of blocking/confusing issues, proportion explaining limits, proportion naming a next action, and the highest-severity issue with its proposed fix. These are blank fields until sessions occur; they are not current results.
