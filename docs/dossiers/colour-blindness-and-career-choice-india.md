## Sources read (30 Oct 2026) and confidence
- Mohamed Ibrahim v. Managing Director, TANGEDCO, Supreme Court, 16 Oct 2023 (judgment PDF on api.sci.gov.in): employer admitted no norm fixing colour vision for Assistant Engineer (para 9, 17); engineer did not fit Act's categories or 40 percent benchmark (paras 20, 25); reasonable accommodation owed (paras 25-26); Junior Assistant offer a palliative (para 26); reinstatement with 50 percent arrears (para 28). High.
- Ashutosh Kumar v. FTII, Supreme Court, 12 April 2022 (LiveLaw report): FTII to admit colour-blind candidates to all courses; colour grading module excluded or elective; other film schools with similar curriculum to follow. Medium-high (secondary report of the judgment).
- MBBS: Supreme Court order 25 March 2017 asking MCI for an expert committee (LiveLaw); Medical Dialogues 1 Aug 2017 (MCI to lift bar; screening dropped for NEET); LiveLaw 25 Sept 2017 (Tripura admissions; committee conclusion 'not and should not be an absolute bar'). Medium; the page says these are 2017 sources and sends the student to current NMC rules.
- Yash Malviya v. UPSC, Delhi High Court, 18 Dec 2025 (judgment PDF): armed forces colour perception guidelines clause 6; Ishihara at first board, Ishihara plus anomaloscope at appellate and review boards; fresh review board ordered. Medium-high (table in the extract was jumbled, so branch-wise test mapping is not claimed).
- Indian Journal of Aerospace Medicine paper quoting IAF manual IAP 4303: military pilot standard (25 of 38 Ishihara plates, lantern at 6 m); civil standard described separately. Medium; DGCA's own document not read, stated on page.
- RRB CEN 05/2024 proforma for Station Master (A-2): Ishihara test, result 'Normal'. High for that post only.
- Merchant navy: training-guide statement only (no DG Shipping text). Low; labelled as a warning, not a rule.
