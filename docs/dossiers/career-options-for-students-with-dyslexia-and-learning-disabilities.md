## Sources read (30 Oct 2026)
- Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, SLD assessment guidelines S.O. 76(E) (4 Jan 2018) as amended by S.O. 4501(E) (9 Dec 2020): NIMHANS Battery; psychiatrist added to approved list; positive test treated as benchmark disability (para 22.4). Read from a state government copy. Confidence: medium-high.
- CBSE circular 24 Jan 2017 (dyslexia: scribe/extra time; one language in Class X) and CBSE manual Dec 2024 (extra time, scribe/reader, computer, calculators; PDF upload up to 2 MB). The two differ on calculators; page tells the student to ask the school. Confidence: medium.
- NTA JEE Main 2025 notice as reported by Careers360 (25 Oct 2024): min. 1 extra hour on 3-hour paper; 20 min/hour with scribe; scribe automatic for blindness, locomotor, CP; others need a certificate of limitation in writing. Confidence: medium (secondary).
- CLAT 2024 notification: +40 min on 2 hours; scribe rules. Confidence: medium.
- ICSI FAQ: dyslexia, dysgraphia, dyscalculia as special cases; spelling leniency, cover stamp, instruction to examiner; 20 min/hour for listed physical/sensory disabilities; apply 45 days ahead. Undated. Confidence: medium-low (flagged on page).
- DEPwD OM 10 Aug 2022 and guidelines 29 Aug 2018 (compensatory time 20 min/hour; scribe with limitation certificate). Confidence: medium (summary).
- Indian Journal of Psychiatry 2024 commentary on SLD certification of adults and uneven state practice. Confidence: medium.
- RPwD Act 2016 sections 32 and 34 (5 percent seats; 4 percent posts with SLD in one reserved group). Confidence: high (already used on hub).
- Supreme Court Oct 2024 and NMC July 2026 guidelines on MBBS: as reported, same wording as hub. Confidence: medium.
