# Dossier: career-options-for-deaf-and-hard-of-hearing-students-india (30 Oct 2026)

Category: career-options. Funnel: TOFU. Depth class: PILLAR (child of the disabilities hub). Author: Allu Vagdevi.

## STEP 0 duplicate check
No existing page on deaf, hard of hearing, sign language, ISL, DISLI or DTISL. Hub and dyslexia pages mention hearing only in passing. Verdict: DISTINCT. Missing concept: which sign-language courses are for deaf students and which are for hearing students, plus communication access.

## Key finding that changed the page
The earlier plan listed the ISL interpreter diploma (DISLI) as a route for deaf students. ISLRTC FAQ and the AYJNISHD 2025-26 notice say DISLI needs hearing in the normal range; only deaf candidates can study DTISL (Diploma in Teaching ISL). The page is built around that distinction and a mistake card warns against paying for DISLI.

## Sources read (30 Oct 2026) and confidence
- ISLRTC DISLI page: 2 years, 90 seats, RCI approved. High. ISLRTC DTISL page: 2 years, intake 120, RCI approved 2018, for deaf trainees. High. ISLRTC FAQ: DISLI eligibility (10+2 at 50 percent, functional hands, normal hearing); only deaf candidates can study DTISL; job not guaranteed; ISL dictionary 10,000 terms. High.
- AYJNISHD admission notification 2025-26: courses, durations, eligibility (B.Ed Spl. Ed. HI, D.Ed, B.ASLP, DTISL needs Certificate of Disability (Deaf), 45 percent). Seat tables partly garbled in the fetch, so seat counts are not printed on the page. Medium-high.
- Specified-disability appendix (AIIMS Mangalagiri copy citing RPwD Act clause 2(zc)): deaf 70 dB, hard of hearing 60-70 dB, both ears, speech frequencies. High.
- CBSE consolidated circular Acad-06/2016 (18 Feb 2016): one compulsory language for hearing and visual impairment in Class X; third-language exemption Classes VI-VIII for speech or hearing defects; compensatory time 60 min on 3-hour paper; request via school head with medical certificate; helpline 1800-11-8002. The circular does not name deaf students in the scribe rule (stated on page). 2019 CBSE circular could not be fetched. Medium.
- Careers360 news (7 July 2022) on MDU Rohtak DISLI/DTISL: ASLI founder's pay estimate (Rs 25,000-45,000 a month; Rs 750-1,000 an hour freelance). Single-source estimate, labelled as such on the page. Low-medium.
- Cambridge University news (17 March 2025, study in Language Policy): 19 percent out of school in 2014, 387 schools. Medium.
- RPwD Section 34 split (1 percent deaf and hard of hearing): already verified on hub page.
