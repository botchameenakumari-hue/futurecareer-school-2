## What commodity pages get wrong or omit
Naukri/Mastersportal/GoodSpace style pages: skill lists by role and "add 10-15 skills" with no evidence format, no proof, little on ATS reality; repeat "7.4 seconds" and "75% rejected" without method; recommend proficiency levels/bars (Naukri BCom sample shows "Excel (Advanced), Tally (Intermediate)"); no trap list; no AI-skills guidance; no per-degree before/after.

## Fact ledger (confidence)
- Jobscan: 384 recruiters Feb-Mar 2025; 76.4% filter by skills first; 99.7%+ use ATS filters; 49.61% hard=soft, 27.01% soft first, 14.29% hard first; communication in 55% of 4.6M listings; 2.5M applications, title match linked to higher interview rate (association, vendor data). HIGH on what the report says; MEDIUM as evidence (vendor).
- Enhancv: n=25 US recruiters; 23 no auto-reject, 2 do; knockout questions; 52% say applying early helps; 8% over two pages. MEDIUM (small, vendor). Page labels it.
- Greenhouse: keyword over-reliance may overlook qualified candidates. HIGH.
- Ladders 2018: 7.4 s; layout findings; ERE critique (measures keep-or-skip look; method not reported). MEDIUM.
- NACE: 8 competencies (Dec 2025 version) incl. communication, critical thinking, teamwork, technology. HIGH.
- Built In: 1,000 US hiring managers; 78% at least somewhat important; 19% very; ranked sixth; 60% want to test/discuss/see proof; 76% AI-written resumes harder to read what candidate did. MEDIUM (sponsor not stated; US).
- TopResume: n=600 US hiring managers; 19.6% reject AI resume; 52% accept proofreading/drafting; 33.5% correctly detected. MEDIUM (sponsored by resume company; labelled).
- Mastersportal: 10-15 skills; 8-12 appears in a GoodSpace search snippet only (page says "guides range"). MEDIUM.
- India Skills Report 2026 via Careers360: employability 56.35%; 100,000+ test takers; in-demand AI, data analytics, cloud, cybersecurity. Only the in-demand skills claim is used. MEDIUM (secondary).
- Skill bars invisible to ATS: asserted by vendors (Hiration says "a risk, not a certainty"), untested. UNVERIFIED, stated as such on page.
- "75% rejected" myth: no clear source. Stated as such.
- UNVERIFIED/not used: skill-only-claimed-without-context parser claim (dev.to), WEF percentages (secondary only), Jobscan 10.6x (not printed).
- No pay figures, no prices, no testimonials. All sample lines use [brackets]; no invented numbers.
