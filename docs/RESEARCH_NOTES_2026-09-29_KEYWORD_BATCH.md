## Exams, dates and eligibility (verify on official portals)
- CBSE Class 10 2026: first exams 15-21 May 2026 per one page; a second board exam and its results were reported (result declared around 18 July 2026). CBSE 2026-27 curriculum for Classes 9-10 reported to make a third language mandatory and offer advanced maths/science options. Basic Maths students allowed Maths in Class 11 (relaxation reported). Sources were news sites, not cbse.gov.in.
- NEET UG 2026: reported cancellation and re-exam on 21 June with results and MCC counselling later; over 22 lakh registrations (news). MBBS seat matrix 2026 reported as about 1,36,939 seats (Careers360) and a revised 13,636-seat item at one site; treat as unverified. Qualifying marks page on Careers360.
- JEE Advanced 2026 results press release at jeeadv.ac.in; JoSAA 2026 seat matrix about 67,323 seats (PW Live); JEE Main 2027 Session 1 dates from Shiksha (secondary).
- CUET UG 2026: held 11-31 May with re-tests 6-7 June; result 23 June 2026; over 15.68 lakh registered; max 5 subjects, 1,250 marks; DU uses CSAS. DU UG Bulletin 2026-27 (du.ac.in) accepts Accountancy/Book Keeping in place of Maths for B.Com (Hons), contradicting a competitor claim.
- CUET PG 2026 (NTA bulletin, 157 subjects); NIMCET 2026 exam 6 June, 60% eligibility, about 1,300 seats; JAM 2026 held 15 Feb 2026, about 3,000 seats across 22 IITs (applications usually close in autumn); UGC NET June 2026 notice (75% rule for four-year degree applies to JRF and PhD only); CSIR NET June 2026 (Biotecnika).
- GATE 2027 (IIT Madras, gate2027.iitm.ac.in): registration opened 2 Sep 2026, closes 5 Oct 2026 (12 Oct with late fee); exams 6, 7, 13, 14, 20, 21 Feb 2027; admit cards from 4 Jan 2027; results 19 Mar 2027; 30 papers, 3-year score validity; new Robotics and Automation paper; DigiLocker registration mandatory for Indian nationals.
- CLAT 2027 (Careers360, unverified): exam 6 Dec 2026, 2-4 pm, offline; registration 3 Aug to 31 Oct 2026; UG eligibility 45% (40% SC/ST); fee Rs 4,000 (Rs 3,500 SC/ST/BPL); about 25 NLUs, 3,649+ UG seats; 120 questions, negative marking 0.25. Supreme Court reviewing Bar Council age cap (25 Sep 2026, Bar and Bench). Do not claim CLAT is run by the Bar Council of India (a competitor error).
- CAT 2026: 29 Nov 2026 at IIM Indore; registration closed; 50% marks (45% reserved); final-year students eligible.
- NID DAT 2027: registration 10 Sep to 30 Nov 2026, prelims 20 Dec 2026, B.Des open to any stream (PW). NIFT 2027 Stage 1 on 10 Jan 2027, form via NTA. UCEED (IIT Bombay) open to any stream; participating IITs Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, Hyderabad and IIITDM Jabalpur.
- IPMAT 2027: tentative dates and 60% Class 10/12 marks (Hitbullseye); IIM Indore confirmed the 2026 test on 4 May 2026 and IPM as a five-year integrated programme; about Rs 6 lakh a year for first three years (Cracku, secondary).
- CA: direct entry to Intermediate for graduates reported at 55% (commerce) or 60% (others) by coaching sites; registration about Rs 18,000; CA Foundation pass rates 15-20% over the last three sittings (caexams.in). CS Executive open to any graduate, skipping CSEET, about Rs 23,900 registration plus Rs 1,500 per exam group (2026, secondary). CMA direct entry for eligible graduates; no fee stated. ICAI/ICSI/ICMAI pages gave no eligibility text.
- CFA (cfainstitute.org): USD 3,520-4,570 for all three levels, about 300 study hours per level, 4,000 work hours over at least 36 months; no Class 12 maths condition listed.
- NISM (nism.ac.in): over 35 exams, some SEBI-mandated, valid about 3 years, fees from Rs 0 to about Rs 3,000. Institute of Actuaries of India ACET page used for actuarial entry.
- NDA (PW, NCA Academy) dates are aggregator-sourced; NCHM JEE 2026 brochure at nchm.gov.in; NCET/ITEP (NTA, NCTE); NCTE draft B.Ed rules and UGC four-year degree rule are proposed or reported, not final.
- RBI Grade B 2026: BankersAdda said applications 29 Apr to 20 May 2026, Oliveboard said TBA; sources disagreed. SEBI Grade A 2026 via Testbook. SSC CGL 2026 vacancies revised from 12,256 to 10,731 (secondary). IBPS PO 2026 vacancies quoted as 7,365 vs 7,565.
- ISRO ICRB recruitment pages (isro.gov.in/ICRB_Recruitment12/13.html, 9); ISRO SC needs BE/BTech at 65%. BEL trainee engineer 2026 via Adda247 (pay not stated as first-year).
- UGC SWAYAM credit directive (July 2026) reported by Careers360 and Free Press Journal; SWAYAM content is free, proctored exam carries a fee (NPTEL exam Rs 1,000 per exam); AICTE internship portal free and gives Academic Bank of Credits credits. NPTEL has over 3,200 courses.
- CGST Rule 83 and the GST practitioner exam (taxtmi, cleartax, gstcentre); GST exam fee not verified.
