### Career and skill relationships

Career guides expose three separate skill sets:

1. Core skills: portable capabilities useful across directions.
2. Specialist skills: abilities tied to the selected role or family.
3. Skills for change: future-ready capabilities such as AI, data, privacy,
   automation, climate, and governance.

When a career is saved, specialist and future-ready skills are linked to that
career. Portable foundation skills are stored independently, deduplicated by
name, and remain available if the career is later removed.

## Deliberate boundaries

The reference pages contain audience-specific narratives, salary examples,
exam advice, and marketing copy. Those were not blindly copied into every
student plan. They were converted into reusable career guidance, actions,
skills, and evidence prompts so the dashboard remains useful for different
students and can support additional careers later.
